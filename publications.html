Fatigue-Resistant s-EMG Hand Gesture Recognition with Lightweight Recurrent–Attention Networks

[1] A. Bagheri Tirtashi and M. Jahed, “Fatigue-Resistant s-EMG Hand Gesture Recognition with Lightweight Recurrent–Attention Networks,” in Proc. 34th International Conference on Electrical Engineering (ICEE), 2026, doi: 10.1109/ICEE70628.2026.11710601.

Research highlight: Investigated recurrent and attention-based models for robust sEMG gesture recognition under simulated fatigue-related signal degradation.

IEEE Xplore ↗DOI ↗
Read more about this paper

This study compared conventional classifiers with convolutional, recurrent, and attention-based models for sEMG hand gesture recognition. Performance was tested under simulated amplitude and spectral changes associated with muscle fatigue. It uses recordings from the same experimental sessions as the multimodal study.

ACCEPTED · AWAITING PUBLICATION · 2026

Robust EEG–sEMG Fusion for Hand Gesture Recognition Using Cross-Attention

[2] A. Bagheri Tirtashi and M. Jahed, “Robust EEG–sEMG Fusion for Hand Gesture Recognition Using Cross-Attention,” 33rd International Conference on Biomedical Engineering (ICBME), 2026, accepted for publication.

Research highlight: Developed cross-attention-based multimodal learning to model complementary information between neural and muscular signals for hand gesture recognition.

Read more about this paper

The framework combines EEG and sEMG using parallel convolutional feature encoders, self-attention, and cross-attention. It was evaluated under clean and simulated fatigue-related signal conditions. The manuscript is accepted and awaiting publication; no DOI is claimed.
